# A personal Field Plan

The Field Plan connects the existing supply, workshop construction, restoration and habitat-care loops with regional projects and town needs. It is guidance, not another quest or resource ledger. Players choose which goal to track, inspect its current requirements, locate the actual target and return to the existing controls to act. There is no forced tutorial or reward for opening a panel.

## Choose a route

Open the Field Plan from the Journal, Town or Field tools. Select a goal and explicitly track it. The HUD then shows its current status, real progress and target; Inspect opens the existing controls and Map selects the corresponding destination. Distances and compass bearings are straight-line measurements, not promises of a navigable route. Existing movement, ground, proximity, host and action-authority checks still apply.

Untrack returns the HUD to its ordinary contextual guidance. Regional and town views can also be explicitly selected. Tracking is personal: it is saved in a separate preview-local preference for each world and, online, each room. It does not write campaign slots, world snapshots, room commands, resource counters or resident state. Reload restores the chosen goal on that device and derives fresh progress from the current save. Save export/import does not carry personal preferences to another device. If browser storage is unavailable, tracking remains in memory and the UI reports that limit.

Already completed work is read from its real receipts. A paid workshop shows its assigned resident and achieved work rather than another payment prompt. A finished workshop exposes the existing four-material repair service and recognizes an undamaged suit. Collected scrap caches stay exhausted; one unpaired cargo piece can be unloaded. Habitat restoration completion does not imply current healthy surplus. Delivered care remains subject to stock capacity and actual resident arrivals; a consumed care portion is an arrival-time commitment, not proof that a complete treatment finished.

Selection carries through supported map and inspector transitions. Choosing a source or habitat does not collect it, move the explorer or pin a goal without an explicit tracking choice. Closing a panel, changing goals and repeated inspection do not advance tasks. Solo panels pause simulation; close them to let construction work, needs and cooldowns advance. Online progression remains owned by the shared world, while each participant may inspect their own guidance.

## A short playable loop

1. Track town supply. Visit the selected finite scrap caches and load cargo through the supply board; ordinary pickup remains a separate choice. Return to Second Life Salvage and deliver pairs for the existing twelve-material donation.
2. Track the west workshop. Follow its board marker, inspect the actual recipe quote, select an available resident and commit shared materials. The default plan costs 31 materials. These are shared stores; guidance does not reserve them.
3. Close solo menus while the resident reaches the existing salvage workbench and performs real prefabrication. The tracked goal reports the assigned worker's current reason and actual craft progress. Return to the completed workshop for its material-funded suit repair when needed.
4. Choose restoration and care whenever desired. Follow a habitat dock, use the real automaton to restore viable growth, collect surplus only when the existing gates allow it, then deliver to Greenlight Apothecary. The plan distinguishes restoration history, present surplus, carried stock and actual clinic commitments.

These routes may be pursued in any order. Existing regional systems, dialogue, town needs and normal resource choices remain available.

## Developer rehearsal

**Developer laboratory → Systems → Field plan · competing goals & tracking** uses disposable production models with bounded seed/scenario setup, explicit steps, reset and text evidence export. The short supply-to-workshop scenario begins with a declared zero-material town store and executes finite source loading, real donation payments, construction acceptance, achieved resident work and the existing repair service. Resident material production continues during the rehearsal, so stock after construction can grow through actual work. Explicit operator placement is labeled model-only; no player traversal, browser rendering or device acceptance is inferred. Care scenarios retain the existing paid restoration preparation and finite transport model.

All 100 residents retain their normal identities and motion owner. The rehearsal cannot write campaign saves or shared rooms. Unsupported stock/reward multipliers and live room tuning are not exposed. Presets contain bounded setup only; evidence identifies source/build, intended and applied inputs, actual actions, guidance and model progress.

The exact thirteen held food/clock files remain excluded. Regional ledgers and their outstanding review are unchanged. Numerical source/emitted, map and worker checks do not establish browser/GPU/touch/device visual acceptance. Existing gateway-crate and fatigue-replay caveats remain; no private Site, live save or hosting/sharing settings are changed.

The workshop now displays five earned construction phases. Supply and care boards map the selected source/habitat or their explicitly chosen delivery station; interacting with a dock or deployed automaton selects its actual habitat. Rejected navigation keeps the open board usable. See CAMPAIGN-LOOP.md for the combined player and developer walkthrough.
