# A connected route through Hearthmere

Open **Journal → Field Plan** (also in Town and Field tools). Choose a goal and explicitly **Track** it; **Inspect** opens its real controls and **Map** selects its current destination. Untrack restores ordinary guidance. Tracking is personal to this device/world/room and resumes after reload; it is not part of exported campaign saves. Completed work is recognized from the existing saved receipts.

## Salvage → workshop → paid repairs

1. In **Town supply**, choose a remaining scrap source and use **Map selected source**. Approach the actual cache on clear ground with your staff lowered, then explicitly **Load** it through the supply board. Ordinary nearby pickup is a separate choice and puts scrap in your normal inventory. Cargo holds four pieces. Keep ordinary scrap/core supplies for other projects if needed; loading all caches is not a required tutorial.
2. **Map delivery station** selects Second Life Salvage. Return there and deliver two cargo pieces for twelve shared town materials. The ordinary donation cooldown lasts ten active seconds; close solo menus for it to advance. An odd piece can be unloaded into normal inventory at the same station. New towns already have 120 materials, so this route replenishes actual demand rather than being a mandatory prerequisite. Existing Visit Hearthmere travel retains cargo and still requires its usual readiness conditions.
3. Track **Construction**, then follow its map to the northwest board at **x −226, z −193**. Apply the bounded recipe to inspect its plan and quote, choose an available resident, and explicitly commit materials. The default recipe costs **31 town materials** and **72 achieved prefabrication seconds**. Editing, applying and importing a recipe do not pay. The parcel is permanent; cancellation, demolition and refunds are not offered.
4. Close solo menus. The assigned resident travels to the actual Second Life workbench and prefabricates there. Their travel, blocked approaches and urgent personal recovery do not earn construction work. Reopen the board to inspect their name, current reason, achieved work and remaining work. The site changes from **funded → frame → panels → finishing → complete** only as this work is earned. Its protective coverings are solid from funding; finishes do not add new collision around you. There is no pretend on-site worker.
5. Once complete, return to the board for **up to 25 suit HP for four shared materials**. Full-health players cannot spend stock. The board explains unavailable repairs and construction requests. The finished geometry, progress and service receipts survive save/reload under the existing validation and co-op authority.

## Restoration → care → actual resident recovery

1. Choose **Restoration** in Field Plan and map a habitat dock. Interacting with a dock or deployed automaton now opens that actual habitat, even after inspecting another one. Use the existing paid body/refit, service, deployment and field-operation controls; step clear of the body before deployment and follow the per-cell conditions. Model-only operator placement in a developer rehearsal is not a campaign travel action.
2. Historical restoration completion does not guarantee present surplus. Open **Habitat care**, select the habitat, and inspect its current gate: every cell must be viable with at least 70 health, collection must leave two biomass per cell, and a collection supplies five portions. The carried capacity is ten. Map the selected habitat and collect only through the explicit, reachable control.
3. Use **Map delivery station** to return to Greenlight Apothecary and deliver. The clinic holds twenty portions. Close solo menus so ordinary residents can choose and reach recovery. One care portion is committed on an actual recovery arrival alongside ordinary material/water costs; the 1.5× recovery benefit applies only during achieved, unobstructed work. Waiting, walking or a consumed portion alone does not prove a completed treatment. The care board reports stock, commitments and current patients.

Shared cargo, care and construction spending remain host-managed. Personal guidance never grants resources or issues work credit. Failed Visit/Map navigation leaves the current board usable, including Close; successful navigation remains owned by the existing panel/world lifecycle. Regional food and weather accounting remain separate.

## If a control is unavailable

Read the action's current gate before retrying. The connected boards distinguish host-only spending, a paused or reconnecting room, unfinished terrain/collision loading, staff or guard recovery, crouching, crate/ledge contact, distance and solid obstructions. Host updates refresh the current stores and gates without reopening the board or applying recipe edits. Close solo menus to move or finish recovery, then return to the same control; terrain acknowledgements also refresh its availability.

A queued request is not proof of payment or completion. A local rejection stays visible instead of becoming “request sent.” Restoration waits while its command remains queued or in flight, even across Recheck, unrelated host updates or closing/reopening. Once the transport settles, inspect the current stores and event record; an unchanged result may be retried only when currently eligible. Background suspension still drops unsent actions and retains an already-sent packet for an exact retry. Closing a board does not cancel an in-flight payment or grant a refund.

Rejected Map/Care navigation leaves restoration controls usable. Dismissed or replaced panels ignore their old callbacks, including delayed recipe reads. Invalid campaign-overlay replies retain the client's last confirmed world and retryable command; they do not partially import resources. Recoverable network failures continue through the existing reconnect path. A closed room or replaced session still requires the existing leave/rejoin flow.
