## Scope of this checkpoint

Water compartments conserve volume and physically change a cave route. They are not a full river/surface/pressure/mixture solver. The optional paid outfall now meters actual cave drainage into the existing household loading depot, with proportional carrier provenance and actual-delivery job evidence. Settlement water, residents and causal jobs continue under their existing conservative rules.

Manufacture consumes finite scrap and real water-supported caretaker service. Natural-wear requests reserve an available kit, have a rest interval and exactly-once finite rewards. Their history and settlement motifs form a bounded factual content pack, not a whole cultural-history simulator.

Online rooms have real shared server state and authenticated membership, with a target of up to four exchanges per second. They are not an MMO or a measured low-latency networking claim. The Site audience remains private unless the owner separately authorizes friends to access it.

No claim is made that all 17 original domains are complete. The next dependency-ordered work is richer encounter/ability content, broader ecology/climate experiments, simulation ownership/tiering and measured regional streaming. Scale should follow a verified valley.
